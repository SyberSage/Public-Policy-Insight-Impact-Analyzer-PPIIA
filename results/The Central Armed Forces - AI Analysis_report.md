## What this means for citizens
- This legislation standardizes how senior officers in central armed security forces are hired, promoted, and managed.
- It directly affects senior officers in forces such as border security, reserve police, and industrial security units.
- Officers from the police service and military serving on temporary assignment with these forces are also affected.
- For the general public, daily routines will not change, as the law deals purely with internal force administration.
- The national government gains explicit authority to set standard rules for officer recruitment, transfers, and working conditions.
- These new service rules will take priority over older administrative guidelines and past court rulings.
- Existing officer recruitment rules will remain valid until the government officially updates or replaces them.
- Half of all Inspector General posts in these forces must be filled by temporary transfers from the police service.
- At least sixty-seven percent of Additional Director General positions will also be set aside for temporary officer transfers.
- The very top command roles, including Director General, will be filled entirely through temporary officer transfers.
- Financial benefits currently received by officers will remain in place until any updated financial orders are issued.
- The government can expand this administrative system to cover other national security forces in the future.
- All new administrative rules created under this framework must be presented to Parliament for oversight.
