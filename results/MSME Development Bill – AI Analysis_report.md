- State governments may also require state public sector enterprises and other specified bodies to settle supplier invoices through the electronic discounting platform.
- Notified public enterprises and entities must publicly disclose details of the micro, small, and medium enterprise invoices they settle through the platform.
- Disputes involving supplier payments can be handled through mediation or arbitration, which now includes options for official online proceedings.
- Facilitation councils or mediation providers must complete mediations within ninety days from the first appearance date.
- Facilitation councils must make an arbitral award within ninety days from the completion of pleadings.
- Mediated settlement agreements and arbitral awards can be recovered as land revenue arrears and are recognized as legally enforceable debts.

- Courts handling such challenges must order partial payouts to the supplier, including at least fifty percent of the awarded amount if the case lasts over six months.
- State governments must establish adequate local councils with balanced representation from industry associations, legal experts, and government officials.
- Providing false registration information or failing to comply with reporting and payment rules will trigger official warnings, penalties, or fines.
- An adjudicating officer will handle inquiries and penalties, with a structured appeals process available to aggrieved parties within specific time limits.
